# v0.8.2

## Choose individual event themes

All three cartridges have five optional Sound selectors: 1-UP, Game Over,
Continue Screen, Act Clear and Invincibility. Each has one dropdown, including
the original Sonic 1 / Sonic 2, Sonic 3, Sonic & Knuckles and supported prototype
variants appropriate to that event. US Sonic CD supplies Game Over, Act Clear
and Invincibility choices. All replacements default off. Disable a feature to
use the game's normal theme; its selected replacement is retained.

Choose a theme and provide its matching ROM or disc if needed. ZIPs import
automatically. A verified donor is stored once and reused by other matching
selectors and normal installations of all three games. The picker follows the
dropdown and shows the selected choice's requirement. Choices remain editable
while disabled; importing the correct donor enables that event automatically.
Invalid or mismatched inputs retain the previous verified donor.

Clone Driver is enabled automatically for replacements. Restart after changing
selections. These options retain its existing local-play/original-campaign
limits. Instruments, envelopes and percussion come from the selected cartridge;
CD themes retain stereo playback and pause during cartridge 1-UP jingles.
Current-build save/load and rewind retain the selected sound configuration and
playback clocks. Unselected music and cartridge sound effects remain available,
and Sonic 3 retains its original Super music when replacing Invincibility.

See [Event music](EVENT_MUSIC.md) for all choices and donor details. No ROM,
recording, sample or other extracted game asset is distributed in the packages.
