# v0.8.7

Widescreen object loading follows the cartridge's allocation range and native
residency flags across bonus returns and act transitions. Flying Battery keeps
its foreground, background and cloud artwork with their correct owners.
Mushroom Hill prototype terrain palettes also apply during native direct palette
copies while retaining the shared object colors.
Hydrocity prototype backgrounds follow the native water-palette scanlines,
including the dry first current tunnel.

The Mushroom Hill ending keeps the ship and propellers together in expanded
views using their native scrolling and presentation period. Standalone Sonic 3
also retains Knuckles' waiting pose on Angel Island's right ledge before the
boss defeat when that ledge is visible in the added margin.

Imported music retains the correct credits key changes, mini-boss requests,
special-stage tempo periods and high PSG notes. The music player separates the
Ending and Super themes and handles held buttons once. Use A or Start to play,
C to stop, B to return, and A+C to pause or resume. Track navigation stops
playback and resets channel selection; channel changes retain the song position.
The opening shortcut remains on the recomp-ui Mods page.

Music Customization adds Sonic 1 Special Stage, Simon Wai Invincibility/Super,
additional Competition menu themes and independent standalone Sonic 3 Super
choices. Stock labels and applicable retail choices preserve saved selections.
See [Music customization](EVENT_MUSIC.md) for controls and required donor ROMs.

The optional **All emeralds in No Save** setting starts original-campaign No Save
runs with seven Chaos Emeralds in Sonic 3 and Sonic & Knuckles, or all fourteen
Chaos and Super Emeralds in Sonic 3 & Knuckles. It is disabled by default and
preserves the earned progress in native save slots.
