# Campaign zone gallery

These PNGs contain real Sonic3KRecomp gameplay captures. Screens retain their
original pixels and full frame; captions and borders are outside the game
image. The gallery uses runtime commit `5c00831`, whose gameplay source is
unchanged in main's `83bf57b` documentation update.

The 46 distinct zones include final boss areas. CD's seven zones have 70
act/time variants; its gallery samples all four periods, rather than picturing
every variant. The native S3K campaign is split into Sonic 3 and Sonic &
Knuckles galleries for readability.

Forty-five zone captures are 398x224 widescreen. The Doomsday is 320x224 native
aspect, explicitly captioned, because the current wide renderer omits Hyper
Sonic. Its native frame is centered without stretching. The other native
campaign frames use widescreen too.

Captures rotate all eight character selections: Sonic & Tails, Sonic, Tails,
Knuckles, Mighty & Tails, Mighty, Shadow & Tails and Shadow. The Doomsday shows
Sonic's Hyper form from the private completed-save fixture. Gallery selections
are recorded in `catalog.json`.

Stage selection, protection and private completed navigation saves were used
to stage the images. These pictures illustrate content and presentation; they
are not additional boss-completion or route-validation evidence. Source ROMs,
disc data, converted packs and saves remain private. Owner saves are untouched.
