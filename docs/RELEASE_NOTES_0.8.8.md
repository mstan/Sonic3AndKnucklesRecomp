# v0.8.8

Expanded views keep background parallax aligned with the displayed camera at
level boundaries in Hydrocity, Carnival Night, Mushroom Hill and Flying Battery.

The cartridge's object loader retains ownership of its normal loading range.
Expanded views no longer reallocate the IceCap bridge after its collapse or
recreate an in-level giant ring during the special-stage entry fade. Bonus
returns retain their native object and collision behavior.

Mushroom Hill uses complete terrain resources while native decompression is
queued. Its boss arena retains the installed foreground and background bands,
and native looping scenery continues through the added margins. The waiting
capsule becomes visible in the expanded view before its native allocation.
Prototype terrain colors retain the shadow used by recessed rock artwork.

Flying Battery excludes layout-copy storage from the visible level and keeps
the outdoor and indoor entrance artwork in their respective regions. Its
authored cloud motion continues independently of the bounded view.

The prototype Competition menu music retains its instrument and detune setup
when its repeated phrase loops. Existing music selections and imported files
remain usable. See [Music customization](EVENT_MUSIC.md) for controls and
required donor ROMs.
