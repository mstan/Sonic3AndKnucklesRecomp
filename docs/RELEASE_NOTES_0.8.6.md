# v0.8.6

Act handoffs use a soft camera pan while retaining the correct outgoing and
incoming scenery. Expanded views retain Hydrocity's incoming background,
Launch Base's approach and rocket scenery, IceCap's exit snow and Turbo Spiker
art after water-current transitions. Ending scenery stops at its authored
boundary. Bonus-stage HUD elements and moving title words retain their layout
across the expanded view.

Music Customization retains Shared Themes and the running game's campaign
section. Data Select and the other common sounds remain available in all three
games. Additional cartridge variants, independent saved selections and explicit
retail overrides remain available. Prototype PSG timing follows native note
boundaries and tempo. CD act-clear recordings can finish across a stage change.

Each music row can import a custom SMPS BIN, supported Sonic 3 SMPS2ASM source,
Genesis VGM/VGZ or PCM WAV file. The title-screen and level-select music player
opens with A+C and supports play, pause, stop and individual synthesized channel
audition without restarting the song. See [Music customization](EVENT_MUSIC.md)
for controls and supported input formats. Game music and donor assets remain
supplied by the player.

Keyboard and controller binding edits, resets and pad-mode changes reach every
supported player. All three packages retain adaptive Fit to screen defaults
and preserve previously saved aspect, sound and mod choices.
