# v0.8.3

Music customization is grouped into Shared Themes, Sonic 3 Campaign and
Sonic & Knuckles Campaign, with a dropdown for each applicable song. Each
zone and act offers Base game and only its own prototype music. Competition,
bonus-stage and menu tracks have individual prototype choices too. The Competition menu has explicit retail/prototype choices; Credits offers both retail cartridges and the prototype.
All tracks default to Base game; existing enabled event choices are retained.

Shared Themes adds drowning, main boss, mini-boss and title replacements.
Main boss and mini-boss choices are independent, including retail Sonic 2
and Simon Wai prototype music. Sonic 3's mini-boss and title themes can be
used in the S&K games, and Sonic 3 can use their counterparts. Prototype
Special Stage, Knuckles and Emerald choices are also available, with a separate Super replacement in standalone Sonic 3.

Corrected the full prototype soundtrack's S&K credits mapping and retained the retail ending where the donor has no corresponding song.

Only donor files needed by the selected choices appear, with one picker per
donor in each section. Verified imports are reused across sections and games.
Disable a section to restore normal music while keeping its choices, or choose
Base game for a single track. Replacements use Clone Driver and apply after
restart during local play in original campaigns. Sound effects are retained.

See [Music customization](EVENT_MUSIC.md) for the choice matrix, donor revisions
and prototype source links. No extracted game assets are distributed.
