# Sonic 3 family v0.5.0 — Widescreen and Knuckles fixes

Windows x64 ZIPs and Linux x86_64 AppImages for **Sonic 3**, **Sonic 3 &
Knuckles**, and **Sonic & Knuckles**. Supply your own matching ROM; none is
included.

## Changes

- Blue Spheres objects now sit at grid corners in widescreen, including during
  movement and turns. Spheres, bumpers and rings share the ground projection.
- Repaired widescreen backgrounds in Flying Battery, Sandopolis and other
  stages, including transitions between background banks. Angel Island's fire
  transition, first miniboss art and hollow-tree reveal are also corrected.
- Tails' title-screen plane and the Knuckles parade extend beyond the original
  4:3 area. Restored the missing red pixels on Knuckles' title-screen hair.
- **Extra Knuckles** now accepts any number from **1–74**, with **16** as the
  default. The cap reserves room for stage objects; busy scenes can use fewer.
- S3K and S&K save states resume the current gameplay moment and restore the
  Knuckles crowd instead of reloading the stage. Use **Shift+F1–F9** to save,
  **F1–F9** to load, or controller **LB/RB** for slot 1.

Widescreen and the Knuckles mod remain opt-in under **Mods**. Choose
**Widescreen → Adaptive** to follow the window. The crowd is local play only.

## Running and upgrading

Extract the complete Windows ZIP and run its EXE. On Linux, put the AppImage
in a writable folder, make it executable (`chmod +x`), and launch it. Use
`--appimage-extract-and-run` when FUSE is unavailable.

Preserve `settings.ini`, `rom-*.cfg`, `*-mods.ini` and `*.srm` when upgrading.
Save states require the same build and mod/widescreen configuration; use normal
cartridge saves (`*.srm`) to carry progress between releases.

## Validation and limits

Validation covers all 13 attract selections across the three games, native
and wide titles, Blue Spheres, empty/existing save files, Knuckles gameplay,
and the packaged launchers. Release checks compare Windows/Linux gameplay
pixels, RAM/VRAM and attract state/audio hashes. The owner approved the Blue
Spheres alignment. This does not certify every stage or boss.

Widescreen remains experimental. A longer IceCap Act 1 route still triggers
an existing development stack-check failure and is tracked separately. Some
objects react only to the original players; monitors break only for Player 1.
The Knuckles crowd is inactive in special/bonus/competition stages and demos.

Linux targets Ubuntu 24.04 (glibc 2.39) or compatible x86_64 systems with OpenGL.
Older distributions and Steam Deck remain unvalidated. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers
all six packages; each includes licenses and exact source/dependency metadata.
