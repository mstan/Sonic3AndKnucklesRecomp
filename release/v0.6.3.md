# Sonic family v0.6.3

Windows x64 ZIPs and Linux x86_64 AppImages for Sonic 3, Sonic & Knuckles,
and Sonic 3 & Knuckles.
Includes the v0.6.2 runtime and these player-reported fixes:

- Widescreen title scenery joins cleanly; Data Select no longer repeats its portrait and text into the margins.
- Failed imported Blue Spheres entrances advance to the next uncollected stage.
- Imported stage backgrounds no longer receive an extra pan at the left edge.
- Submerged elemental-shield monitors in imported Sonic 1/2 stages contain bubble shields, including existing converted packs.
- Shadow and companion Tails run off during ordinary imported act-clear tallies; Tails' separate boundary check permits his exit. Control-restoring tallies retain their cutscenes.
- Shadow's native ending hold no longer plays the bubble/walking script. His complete head fits both the life icon and 1-UP monitor.

**Replayable feedback is on by default**, including upgrades from settings that
predate this option. Reports stay on your machine. After a bug, quit normally
and send the game's `logs/*-feedback.replay` plus its `.sav`, `.config.ini` and
`.mods.txt` sidecars with a description. Nothing uploads automatically.
Mods > Diagnostics can disable recording. See `docs/PLAYER_FEEDBACK.md`;
`tools/replay_diagnostic.py` is included for reproducing reports.

The intermittent GHZ1 signpost hang remains unconfirmed and is not claimed
fixed. Newly reported Blue Spheres widescreen/Shadow presentation, Labyrinth
water/control/art/platform problems (including the LZ2 progression blocker),
and Competition-menu wrapping remain for the next update. Campaign additions
remain experimental.

Bring your own matching game media. No game media, converted packs, recordings,
personal saves or settings are distributed. Preserve native saves and campaign
companions when upgrading. Build metadata identifies the exact source pins.
