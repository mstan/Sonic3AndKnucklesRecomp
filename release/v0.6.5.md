# Sonic family v0.6.5

Windows x64 updates for Sonic 3, Sonic & Knuckles and Sonic 3 & Knuckles.
Includes the current main-branch game fixes from v0.6.4 and pins the shared
engine to merged optimization-campaign commit 20c8272b0891435f2be60275b38ce288aee2205d
(core 03ba2787da829369bbc2d958621b10330885c8f8).

- Carries the qualified execution, persistence, capture and rendering ownership improvements, including optional GPU high-resolution presentation.
- Sonic 3 & Knuckles uses qualified native IPO/profile-guided optimization. Sonic 3 and Sonic & Knuckles retain ordinary compilation: their IPO-only experiments were rejected; ordinary-compilation timing was neutral/inconclusive under shared-host variation. No universal speedup or zero-overhead claim is made.
- Keeps current-build rewind/save-load, native saves, clean gameplay captions and replayable feedback. Native saves and campaign companion files must be preserved when upgrading.

Bring your own matching game media. Public archives exclude game media,
converted packs, recordings, personal saves and settings. Private local owner
archives include only the matching owned ROM in addition to the verified public
payload. Build metadata records exact title, engine, UI and disassembly pins.

Preserves current native/imported palette and menu features through the reviewed
shared-engine presented-raster compatibility contract; renderer callbacks use
designated fields so viewport ownership and capture requests remain distinct.
