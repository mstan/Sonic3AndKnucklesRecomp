# Sonic family v0.7.2

Infinite time now counts past 9:59, up to 99:59, using the original digit artwork. At 99:59 the displayed clock stops, but play can continue without Time Over.

The original 100,000-point time bonus applies only during the actual 9:59 second. Finishing at 10:00 or later receives the normal late-finish bonus of 100 points. Turning the time limit back on after ten minutes restores the native Time Over behavior on the next active timer update.

The option remains off by default. Native/widescreen HUD positions, high-FPS presentation, saved clock values and unrelated timers retain their normal behavior. No new assets. All three S3-family variants retain default-on diagnostic replays and v0.7.1's fixes and supported media imports.

Supply your own matching game media; public packages exclude ROMs, converted content, saves and settings.
